## 摘要
近年来，生成建模的进展显著提升了时间序列生成能力，但现有方法大多要么假设规则的时间网格，要么关注给定采样结构下的特征动态。这使它们不适合以原生形式生成不规则时间序列，因为此时模型不仅需要捕获特征取值，还需要捕获观测发生的数量、发生的时间以及哪些特征被共同观测。为解决这一异构生成问题，我们提出 ChronoFlow，一个按统计粒度组织的统一分层流匹配框架。遵循由粗到细的层次结构，ChronoFlow 首先生成观测数量和逐特征频率，然后联合生成观测时间与特征共观测模式，最后在已实现的模式条件下生成取值。这将复杂的联合生成问题转化为结构对齐的子问题，同时保留其依赖关系。为评估完整的不规则时间序列生成，我们引入了涵盖样本真实性、采样结构、数值保真度以及时间与跨特征依赖的互补指标，并通过受控扰动对其进行验证。在五个基准上，ChronoFlow 在生成保真度上相较现有基线取得了显著提升，同时因子分解研究也支持所提出的层次结构。我们的代码见 https://anonymous.4open.science/r/ChronoFlow。

## Abstract
Recent advances in generative modeling have substantially improved time series generation, yet most existing methods either assume a regular temporal grid or focus on feature dynamics under a given sampling structure. This makes them illsuited for generating irregular time series in their native form, where a model must capture not only feature values, but also how many observations occur, when they occur, and which features are observed together. To address this heterogeneous generation problem, we propose ChronoFlow, a unified hierarchical flow matching framework organized by statistical granularity. Following a coarse-to-fine hierarchy, ChronoFlow first generates observation counts and feature-wise frequencies, then jointly generates observation times and feature co-observation patterns, and finally generates values conditioned on the realized pattern. This turns a complex joint generation problem into structurally aligned subproblems while preserving their dependencies. To evaluate complete irregular time series generation, we introduce complementary metrics spanning sample realism, sampling structure, value fidelity, and temporal and cross-feature dependencies, and validate them through controlled corruptions. Across five benchmarks, ChronoFlow achieves strong improvements in generation fidelity over existing baselines, while factorization studies support the proposed hierarchy. Our code is available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/ChronoFlow.
