## 摘要
地震序列预测需要模型能够学习非线性历史依赖性，同时保持稳健的统计结构。我们开发了一种基于缩放定律的神经标记点过程，称为Fusion，它将目录历史的神经表示与从流行病型余震序列模型导出的时间特征以及从古腾堡-里希特定律导出的震级信息相结合。该模型将应用于输入目录的震级截断与固定的目标事件阈值分开，允许较低震级的地震为预测提供信息，而不改变目标事件集。对于2016-2017年Amatrice-Visso-Norcia序列，当保留较低震级事件时，Fusion在目标事件时间似然上达到最高，优于ETAS和纯神经点过程基线。逐事件和累积分析显示，在Visso和Norcia序列的相当大一部分中，时间增益持续存在。在五个基准目录中，使用固定ETAS先验的目录特定神经训练在最小评估震级截断处产生最高的时间似然。震级似然在基于古腾堡-里希特的ETAS参考之外没有显示出一致的预测增益，表明Fusion捕获的额外信息主要是时间性的。这些结果表明，较低震级的目录历史和经验缩放定律信息补充了神经序列学习对目标事件时间的预测。

## Abstract
Earthquake sequence forecasting requires models that can learn nonlinear history dependence while retaining robust statistical structure. We develop a scaling-law-informed neural marked point process, termed Fusion, that combines neural representations of catalog history with temporal features derived from the Epidemic-Type Aftershock Sequence model and magnitude information derived from the Gutenberg--Richter law. The model separates the magnitude cutoff applied to the input catalog from the fixed target-event threshold, allowing lower-magnitude earthquakes to inform forecasts without changing the target-event set. For the 2016--2017 Amatrice--Visso--Norcia sequence, Fusion achieves the highest target-event temporal likelihood when lower-magnitude events are retained, outperforming both ETAS and a purely neural point-process baseline. Event-wise and cumulative analyses show sustained timing gains through substantial portions of the Visso and Norcia sequences. Across five benchmark catalogs, catalog-specific neural training with a fixed ETAS prior yields the highest temporal likelihood at the minimum evaluated magnitude cutoff. Magnitude likelihood shows no consistent predictive gain beyond the Gutenberg--Richter-based ETAS reference, indicating that the additional information captured by Fusion is primarily temporal. These results show that lower-magnitude catalog histories and empirical scaling-law information complement neural sequence learning for target-event timing.
