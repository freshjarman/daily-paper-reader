## 摘要
本文研究神经跳跃常微分方程（Neural Jump ODEs）在无限维函数空间中的推广。特别地，底层过程 $X$ 现在取值于 $L^2(Ξ, \mathbb{R}^{d_X})$ 而非 $\mathbb{R}^{d_X}$，算子 NJ-ODE 通过生成条件期望的一个代表元来近似该过程的最优预测器。NJ-ODE 模型是一个在线学习连续时间随机过程最优预测的框架，给定离散的、可能不规则且不完整的过去观测。在一系列工作中，该模型已被扩展以处理一般路径依赖过程、带观测噪声和依赖观测、长期预测以及输入输出系统。然而，在这些工作中，底层过程均被限制为有限维的。特别是，函数值问题（如收益率曲线或波动率曲面预测）只能通过离散化处理，而这本质上会导致信息损失。在本工作中，我们借鉴神经算子方法的思想，将 NJ-ODE 框架推广到无限维输出过程。为了证明 NJ-ODE 收敛到最优预测过程，我们发展了一种新的近似策略，该策略同时通过显著放宽假设，推广了之前在有限维设定下的工作。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study the extension of Neural Jump ODEs to infinite-dimensional function spaces. In particular, the underlying process $X$ now takes values in $L^2(Ξ, \mathbb{R}^{d_X})$ instead of $\mathbb{R}^{d_X}$ and the Operator NJ-ODE approximates the optimal predictor of this process by producing a representative of the conditional expectation. The NJ-ODE model is a framework for online learning the optimal prediction of continuous-time stochastic processes, given discrete, possibly irregular and incomplete past observations. In a series of works, this model has been extended to deal with generic path-dependent processes, with observation noise and dependent observations, with long-term predictions, and with input-output systems. However, throughout all of these works, the underlying processes were restricted to be finite-dimensional. In particular, function-valued problems, like yield curve or volatility surface predictions, could only be handled through discretization, which inherently leads to a loss of information. In this work, we build on ideas from Neural Operator methods that allow us to extend the NJ-ODE framework to an infinite-dimensional output process. To prove convergence of the NJ-ODE to the optimal prediction process, we develop a new approximation strategy that also generalizes previous works in the finite-dimensional setting by considerably weakening the assumptions.
